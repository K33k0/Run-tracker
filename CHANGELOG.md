# RunZone changelog

## v1.2.9

- Training load and readiness: every run with heart rate gets a load score. The run list shows fitness, fatigue and form over 12 weeks, with a daily verdict (Fresh, Balanced, Building, Take it easy or Overreaching). The run screen shows today's verdict.
- Morning HRV check: 2 minutes lying still with your strap on. It measures resting HRV and heart rate, and readiness compares each morning with your last week.
- Zone suggestions: RunZone finds your maximum heart rate from your runs, and your aerobic threshold from steady runs where possible, then suggests zone boundaries you can apply in one tap. Maximum and resting heart rate can also be set by hand.
- Charts are interactive: drag to read values, pinch to zoom, double-tap to reset.
- Back up all runs, HRV checks and training settings to one file, and restore them on any phone. Account sign-ins are never included.
- Redesigned Settings, grouped into Your heart, During a run, Auto-pause, Your data, Sensor & phone, and About, with on/off switches beside each option.

## v1.2.8

- Fix zone beeps sounding doubled while music is playing.
- New run analysis on each run's detail page: metres per heartbeat, grade-adjusted pace (overall, per kilometre, and charted with elevation), rolling 60-second efficiency, aerobic decoupling, time in zone, cadence and estimated step length, heart-rate variability (RMSSD and DFA α1) during steady effort, and best efforts within the run.
- Record heart rate for 2 minutes after finishing while the sensor stays connected, and show heart-rate recovery at 1 and 2 minutes.
- Compare runs on the same route: grade-adjusted pace and efficiency for every run of that course.
- Trends on the run list: weekly distance, and efficiency, grade-adjusted pace and decoupling across your last 12 weeks.

## v1.2.7

- Pause, resume, or finish and save a run from its notification, including on the lock screen.
- Delete runs from their detail page. Copies already uploaded to Smashrun, Strava, or Runalyze are kept there.
- Keep distance, duration, and pace on screen during a run without scrolling.
- Show current pace over the last 30 seconds alongside average pace.
- Buzz and announce each kilometre with its split time. Turn this off in Settings.
- Show long runs as hours, minutes, and seconds in the run list.

## v1.2.6

- Automatically save the run in progress every 30 seconds. If RunZone is closed unexpectedly, the next launch offers to keep or discard the unfinished run.
- Save battery by checking for zone beeps only while a run is recording with zone beeps turned on.
- Show the same default heart-rate zone in Settings and on the run screen.

## v1.2.5

- Save, share, and sync runs as FIT files for Smashrun, Strava, and Runalyze.
- Include automatic kilometre laps, GPS speed, active and elapsed time, estimated moving time, and elevation gain and loss when data is available.
- Record pause and resume times directly on new runs.
- Record cadence from compatible phone step sensors and beat-to-beat intervals from compatible Bluetooth heart-rate straps.

## v1.2.4

- Ask for missing location, Nearby devices, and notification permissions when RunZone opens, where supported by Android. RunZone accepts approximate location if you choose it.
- Match Smashrun’s public-client callback to the active sign-in even when the response omits OAuth state, as long as the callback includes the matching attempt identifier.
- Show specific messages for expired, declined, or mismatched Smashrun sign-ins and missing upload permission.
